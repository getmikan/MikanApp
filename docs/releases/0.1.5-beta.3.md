Mikan **0.1.5 beta.3**, build **10201028**.

- **App proxies:** Rules → App proxies. Add your own SOCKS5 or HTTP proxies (a pasted `socks5://user:pass@host:port` or `host:port:user:pass` link fills the form) and send chosen apps through one of them, through a server or group of the subscription, straight past the VPN, or block them. A proxy connects through the VPN unless you turn that off, and its password is encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore. Needs Android 10 or newer and the VPN mode.
- **Blocked, never leaked:** if an app's proxy cannot be used or does not answer, or its server is gone from the subscription, that app is blocked instead of falling back, and the screen says why. **Check** measures your proxies. Apps with a route always stay inside the VPN, even when App Routing leaves them out.
- **Kill switch:** Settings → Network → Security → Kill switch. If the VPN stops on its own (for example, a profile that fails to load), Mikan keeps an empty tunnel up, so no app reaches the network until you tap **Reconnect** or **Disconnect** in the notification. The same section shows whether Android's own "Block connections without VPN" is on and opens the VPN settings: only that covers Mikan being closed by Android and reboots.
